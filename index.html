About me

I'm a Computer Science & Information Technology student at REVA University. I learn best by building something real and then fixing it until it actually holds up.

The projects I care about are the ones somebody leans on: an app an elder can use with the Wi-Fi off, a PDF reader that explains the hard paragraph, a vault that hands your digital life to the right person.

I work across the stack — Flutter and React on the front, Node, Express and Postgres behind it — and I like it when the details people never notice are the ones that got the most care.

NIRVANA

An offline-first companion and care app for elders, with a live portal for the family caregiver. Reminders, memory games and preferences all keep working with the network unplugged, then sync the moment a connection returns.

  • Exact on-device reminders with Done · Snooze · Later right from the notification
  • Three gentle memory games that never say "failed"
  • One-tap "I'm lost" location sharing and a caregiver dashboard
  • Voice in and out, in English, हिन्दी, বাংলা, ଓଡ଼ିଆ and more

Flutter · Dart · Hive · PostgreSQL · Node

VENMA AI PDF Viewer

A reading desk with a companion. Open a PDF, highlight as you go, and select any passage to ask VENMA — a lavender cat who lives on your desk — for an explanation, an example, a summary or study notes.

  • Highlights and notes saved in the browser, re-attached when the PDF reopens
  • Answers can be pinned to the page as notes
  • No API keys in the extension: a small local server picks a reachable model (Ollama, OpenRouter, Groq, Gemini)
  • The cat breathes, blinks and follows your cursor — and holds still for reduced motion

React · TypeScript · pdf.js · pdf-lib · IndexedDB · Express

DIGIPASS

A digital estate manager. Store your accounts, name trusted executors, and let DIGIPASS generate a digital will — with a dead man's switch that delivers it if you stop checking in.
